Calculator Help

Amount required (net) $
The net amount you need (e.g. construction funds). When a facility fee % is set, the calculator grosses up to the facility size so that after the fee, the remainder covers this amount plus interest.
Interest rate (% p.a.)
The nominal annual rate as quoted by the lender. Enter as a percentage (e.g. 12 for 12%).
Capitalisation frequency
How often interest is added to the balance (capitalised).
Facility term (months)
Total length of the facility in months.
Utilisation during draw (%)
Average percentage of the facility limit that is in use during the construction/draw period.
Facility fee (%)
Upfront fee as % of the facility limit (e.g. 3%).
Effective annual rate
The true yearly rate after capitalisation.
